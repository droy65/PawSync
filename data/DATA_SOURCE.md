# Data source
Real records from the **Austin Animal Center** (City of Austin open data), as published in the Kaggle
"Shelter Animal Outcomes" competition. 26,729 labeled records (outcome known, Oct 2013 - Feb 2016) and 11,456 unlabeled
records. Columns: Name, DateTime, OutcomeType, AnimalType, SexuponOutcome, AgeuponOutcome, Breed, Color.
Nothing in this project is randomly generated. Fields the dataset does not contain (size, temperament, health score,
vaccination, fees, days in shelter, capacity) are deliberately NOT modelled or shown.
